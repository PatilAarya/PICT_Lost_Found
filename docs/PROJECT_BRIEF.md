# Project brief

PICT Lost and Found is for PICT students, staff, the security desk and admins. People report lost items, post found items, get matched automatically, prove ownership, and collect the item. Every step is recorded.

Modules: (1) Lost: lost report with category, description, optional photo, last-seen place and time window; place chosen by tapping a floor plan; match engine ranks found items with a plain-language "why this match"; live status notifications; saved items for fast reporting. (2) Found: camera capture of several photos; in-browser colour and category suggestion (TensorFlow.js); auto-blur of screens and text in the public copy while the clear original stays private; category-specific hidden-detail questions; smart drop-off suggestion to the nearest desk; history. (3) Verify and Handover: owner check with hidden-detail questions, 3 attempts then a lockout; handler makes the final decision; 6-digit one-time handover code (no QR); tamper-evident hash-chain custody log; lifecycle rules (24 hours at the desk, then admin office; donate after 30 days; reminders); analytics dashboard; abuse and spam flagging with admin review; audit log.

Design rules: concept is a lost-property CLAIM TICKET (punched hole, serial number on a stub, perforated edge, rubber-stamp status). NO emojis. NO Lucide, Font Awesome, Heroicons or Material icons. Phosphor (thin weight) only for about 15 small control icons. Category pictograms, status stamps and empty-state illustrations are custom SVG in one consistent style. Avoid generic AI-site looks: no purple-blue gradients, no glassmorphism, no rows of identical rounded soft-shadow cards, no big gradient hero headline, no marketing copy ("seamless", "effortless", "reunite with your belongings"). Copy is sentence case, specific and plain; errors say what to do next. More visuals (photos, pictograms, stamps, timelines) and less text. Accessible: visible focus, contrast, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion respected.
